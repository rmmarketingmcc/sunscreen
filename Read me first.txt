SUNSCREEN

From sunset, your screens lose their blue light and an external monitor
dims. In the morning everything goes back to normal. Automatic, every day.

INSTALL
1. Drag Sunscreen into the Applications folder.
2. Open Sunscreen from Applications.
   macOS says it can't verify the app (it's not from the App Store).
   Click "Done".
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Sunscreen. Confirm with your password.
   You only do this once.

A small sun appears in the menu bar (a moon in the evening). That's it.
It starts by itself when you log in.

NEED A BREAK? (photo editing, a movie, anything)
Press Control + Option + Command + P anywhere to pause for 1 hour.
Press it again to switch it back on. Or click the sun/moon and pick
"Pause for 15 minutes", "Pause for 1 hour" or "Pause for tonight".
It switches back on by itself when the pause runs out.

Click the sun or moon to open the panel. It shows where you are, the
local time, today's sunrise and sunset, and when the filter turns on.
There you can also:
- pick the evening colour. "Sleep" (no blue, like anti-blue-light
  glasses) is recommended. Or drag the slider.
- set the brightness of each screen; daytime and evening are remembered
  separately, so it dims in the evening and comes back in the morning
- pick the daytime colour ("Daylight" is recommended)
- click "Use exact location" for exact sunset times (by default your
  Mac's time zone is used; macOS asks for permission once)
- click the eye to preview the evening look for 10 seconds

The evening colour works on every screen. Brightness works directly on
the MacBook screen and most external monitors; if a monitor doesn't allow
it, Sunscreen dims it in software instead.

SOMETHING NOT WORKING?
Click the sun > Report a problem. All the technical details are filled in,
you just press Submit (needs a free GitHub account).

UPDATES
When there's a new version, a small dot appears on the sun and the
panel says "Update available". Click Update and it installs itself.

REMOVE
Click the sun or moon > Quit Sunscreen, then drag Sunscreen from
Applications to the Trash. Colours go back to normal immediately.
