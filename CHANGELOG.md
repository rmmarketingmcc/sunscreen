# Changelog

Every Sunscreen release. The app shows the newest entry after it updates itself.

## 1.3 (2026-10-21)
- A brightness slider for every screen, MacBook included. Daytime and evening levels are remembered per screen
- Works with far more monitors: software dimming when a monitor can't be dimmed over the cable, and the evening colour as an overlay on DisplayLink, Sidecar and AirPlay screens
- Never overrides the brightness you set yourself
- Warns you when another colour app (like f.lux) fights with Sunscreen instead of flickering
- Normal colours automatically in Photoshop, Lightroom, Final Cut and similar apps
- Spots problems by itself and lets you report them with one click

## 1.2.1 (2026-10-21)
- Checked that automatic updates work

## 1.2 (2026-10-21)
- First release: no blue light in the evening, dimmer monitors, follows sunrise and sunset where you are
- Pause any time with Control-Option-Command-P
- Panel with your local time, sunrise, sunset and when the filter turns on
- Updates itself
