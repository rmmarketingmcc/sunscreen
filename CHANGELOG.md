# Changelog

Every Sunscreen release. The app shows the newest entry after it updates itself.

## 1.13 (2026-10-22)
- The % blue light and every other value in the panel update live while you drag or click; cleaner evening slider

## 1.12 (2026-10-22)
- Sliders are real macOS sliders now, so dragging always works

## 1.11 (2026-10-22)
- Updates always finish: if the app can't quit by itself after updating, it quits hard so the new version starts

## 1.10 (2026-10-22)
- Evening colour is one slider from Max sleep to Natural, sliders work again when the panel scrolls, and picking a colour in the evening ends Bright so it sticks

## 1.9 (2026-10-21)
- Own timing without the 3-hour limit: push the evening past midnight or the morning as late as you like, and hold − or + to move fast

## 1.8 (2026-10-21)
- Your own timing: under All settings, start the evening (or the morning) earlier or later than the sun, in 15-minute steps
- The panel never gets taller than your screen: it scrolls instead of being cut off at the top
- The What's new card is more compact

## 1.7 (2026-10-21)
- Simple view by default: just Auto, Bright, Dark and how much blue light. All settings fold out with one click

## 1.6 (2026-10-21)
- New Auto, Bright and Dark buttons at the top: one click makes it bright or dark right now, and it goes back to Auto by itself
- Every button reacts the moment you click it, and picking a colour shows you the result for a few seconds
- The Start at login and photo-app switches work when you click their text too
- Daytime colour now sits above Brightness, the preview eye is gone (use Dark instead)

## 1.5 (2026-10-21)
- Clicking Sunscreen in the Dock, Spotlight or Applications opens the panel, just like the sun in the menu bar

## 1.4 (2026-10-21)
- Every change now fades gently over 1.6 seconds: colour, brightness, pausing, previews, starting and quitting
- Evening colour in plain words: from Max sleep (0% blue light) to Natural (75%), no more Kelvin
- Shows what's new after every update, and the website keeps a list of every update

## 1.3 (2026-10-21)
- A brightness slider for every screen, MacBook included. Daytime and evening levels are remembered per screen
- Works with far more monitors: software dimming when a monitor can't be dimmed over the cable, and the evening colour as an overlay on DisplayLink, Sidecar and AirPlay screens
- Never overrides the brightness you set yourself
- Warns you when another colour app (like f.lux) fights with Sunscreen instead of flickering
- Normal colours automatically in Photoshop, Lightroom, Final Cut and similar apps
- Spots problems by itself and lets you report them with one click

## 1.2.1 (2026-10-21)
- Checked that automatic updates work

## 1.2 (2026-10-21)
- First release: no blue light in the evening, dimmer monitors, follows sunrise and sunset where you are
- Pause any time with Control-Option-Command-P
- Panel with your local time, sunrise, sunset and when the filter turns on
- Updates itself
